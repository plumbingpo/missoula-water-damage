Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Raw sewage contains dangerous bacteria, viruses, parasites and other pathogens, including E. coli, hepatitis, salmonella and giardia, that pose serious health risks to anyone who touches contaminated materials. Missoula Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Missoula, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

What a Professional Brings to Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. A certified restoration company follows proper disposal protocols and works with licensed facilities that accept biohazard waste.

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Missoula

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Tubs, toilets or floor drains pushing black water back into the room
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • Damp patches on a wall or floor that also smell foul

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • Flush nothing but toilet paper, because wipes, even 'flushable' ones, cause clogs
