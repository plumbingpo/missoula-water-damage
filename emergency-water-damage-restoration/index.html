Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Missoula

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
